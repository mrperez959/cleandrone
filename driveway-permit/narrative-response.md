## Issue #2 — Show separations between utilities, trees, and driveways; show separation between driveways; clean up overlapping labels

> **Reviewer:** *"Show the utilities around the driveway location and indicate
> the distance between them. Minimum allowed: 3 ft of separation between
> utilities, trees, and the driveway; and 1 ft minimum separation between
> the water meter and the driveway. Show in the drawing the separation
> between driveways. Minimum allowed: 10 ft, including neighboring driveways.
> The distance shall be measured at the EOP between the tips of the flares.
> The drawing contains object identifications (figures) and notes placed on
> top of one another, which makes the plan difficult to read and interpret."*

### Response

All separations are now dimensioned directly on the plan view, and all labels
have been moved off-feature with leader lines so that no text overlaps any
other text or symbol.

**Separations dimensioned on Sheet 1:**

| Separation | County Min. | Shown | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| DW1 ↔ DW2 at EOP (between edges; rectangular sections — no constructed flares — see clarification below) | 10 ft | 10 ft | MEETS ✓ |
| WM to DW1 edge | 1 ft | 3 ft | MEETS ✓ |
| Electric Box to DW2 edge | 3 ft | 3 ft | MEETS ✓ |
| Any utility / tree / structure to driveway | 3 ft | 3 ft min. maintained | MEETS ✓ |
| Neighboring driveway to DW1 at EOP | 10 ft | 10 ft | MEETS ✓ |

**Clarification on "between flare tips":** the existing driveways are
**rectangular sections with no constructed flares**. Because no flare exists,
the 10 ft separation is measured between the straight edges at EOP, which is
the most conservative reading of the County requirement (no flare to extend
into the gap). Construction Note 3 on Sheet 2 has been revised to make this
explicit.

**Label legibility:** every callout on Sheet 1 now uses an off-feature
position with a thin leader line. The previous overlaps on the Water Meter
and Electric Box callouts ("WM" / "WATER METER" / "min. 1'" stacked, and
"ELEC BOX" / "ZBox" stacked) have been eliminated. Each label appears once,
in its own location, with a leader pointing to the asset.

**Neighboring driveway:** the neighboring driveway is now shown
schematically (dashed) on Sheet 1 so that the 10 ft separation at EOP is
visually verifiable.
