## Item #11 — two driveways on a 59 ft frontage

This is the only point where the applicant should be ready to provide
additional justification if the reviewer raises it on the next round
(it was not raised in the initial three issues, but is worth pre-empting):

- LDC §6.04.04 generally limits residential lots to one driveway connection
  except where a second access is justified (corner lot, circular drive,
  pre-existing condition, etc.).
- The two driveways at 8506 Seven Coves Ct are **pre-existing** at the time
  of the December 22, 2025 property purchase. The application is filed under
  the **after-the-fact** ROW process specifically to legalize this
  pre-existing condition.
- The two driveways are separated by 10 ft of sod (meets the 10 ft minimum)
  and by their physical configuration function as a circular / split-access
  arrangement on a cul-de-sac lot, which is a recognized residential
  configuration.
- Suggested supporting evidence to attach to the Rev. 1 submittal if needed:
  - Closing-date photo(s) of the existing driveways (shows pre-existence
    on or before 12/22/2025).
  - Aerial photo (Hillsborough County GIS / Google Earth historical) showing
    the two driveways pre-dating 2025.
  - Property survey if available.
