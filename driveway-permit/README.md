question in this round, but it is the most likely topic of a round-2 comment
under LDC §6.04.04 (which generally allows one driveway per single-family
residential lot). The pre-existing-condition argument plus the supplemental
evidence above is the standard justification. See `standards-verification.md`
§"Item #11" for full discussion.
